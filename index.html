What we do

Selling to defence canteens and government buyers has its own rules, portals and paperwork. We take you through each one.

CSD product listing

Preparing your application to introduce products with the Canteen Stores Department: product details, pricing, quality documents and samples, and following it through.

Unit-run canteen supply

Supplying listed products to unit-run canteens (URCs) and defence establishments, with stock, delivery and billing managed by our distribution team.

GeM registration & catalogue

Seller registration on the Government e-Marketplace, vendor assessment, product catalogue upload and responding to bids and direct orders.

Government tender bidding

Finding suitable tenders on central, state and PSU portals, reading the terms, preparing technical and price bids and submitting on time.

Documents & compliance

GST, Udyam (MSME), PAN and bank documents, EMD and performance guarantee, declarations and certificates that tenders ask for.

Distribution partnership

For brands entering our region: warehousing, retail and institutional distribution, home delivery and collections through our own team.
