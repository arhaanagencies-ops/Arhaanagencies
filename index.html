Our story

We began with a simple belief: good products deserve a good road to the customer.

Arhaan Agencies grew out of a familiar problem. Excellent products reach the market, and many never reach the right shelf, because the path from the factory to the customer is long, uneven and full of unseen detail. A missed delivery, an unanswered query or a mistake on an invoice can undo months of a brand’s hard work.

We set out to be the part of that journey that does not fail. On the distribution side, that means disciplined routes, our own delivery people, honest billing and a relationship with every outlet that lasts longer than a single order. We work with brands that take pride in what they make, such as Dukes, Varun Beverages, Country Delight and Troogood, and we treat their name on a crate as our own.

The same care runs through our consultancy. Selling to defence canteens and government buyers has its own rules, portals and patience. We help suppliers understand them, prepare what is needed, and see the process through, from the first application to the first supply.

Today, Arhaan Agencies serves traders, institutions and families alike, from a wholesale order to a morning delivery of milk. Our promise has not changed since the first day: do what we say, on the day we say it, and keep the record straight.

What we do

Marketing consultancy for government and defence supply

Selling to canteens and government buyers has its own rules, portals and paperwork. We take you through each one.

01

CSD product listing

Your application to the Canteen Stores Department: product details, pricing, quality documents and samples, followed through to a decision.

02

Unit-run canteen supply

Supplying listed products to unit-run canteens and establishments, with stock, delivery and billing managed by our distribution team.

03

GeM registration & catalogue

Seller registration on the Government e-Marketplace, a clean product catalogue and help with buyer orders.

04

Government tender bidding

Reading the tender, preparing the bid, arranging EMD and following up with the buyer.

05

Documents & compliance

GST, FSSAI, quality certificates and the other papers buyers ask for, organised and kept current.

06

Distribution partnership

Where it makes sense, we distribute your product ourselves, from the first order to payment follow-up.
